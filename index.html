Choose a colour from the palette, then an empty cell. Choosing a cell that is already filled, or pressing anywhere away from the board and these controls, puts the colour down again without changing anything. The arrow keys move, 1 to 9 place a colour and Backspace clears. The rest of the shortcuts are listed under the board, and every one of them can be moved to a different key from the settings sheet.

How a cell is drawn

Draw the post-it shape that belongs to the number, or flood the cell with its colour.

Symbols on the colours

A second cue on every cell, for when the cell is flooded with colour — in shape mode the shape is already the symbol. Helpful with the grey scale palette, or any time colours alone are hard work.

Keyboard shortcuts

Pick a shortcut, then press the key you want it on — Esc gives up on the change. Any key will do, including the digits and Tab; taking a key from another shortcut leaves that one unset until you give it a key of its own. Binding Tab does cost you the usual way of stepping out of the board to the buttons.
